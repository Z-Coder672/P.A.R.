Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

P.A.R. ("P.A.R.", "we", "us", or "our") is a personal, non-commercial art project. P.A.R. is an interactive pixel-art robot: you draw or upload pixel art on this website, we send it to a physical flip-disc display, a camera records the display drawing your art, and that recording is published to YouTube. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Your artwork. The pixel-art image you create or upload, so we can display it on the physical flip-disc device and show it in the public gallery.
  • Piece name. A name for your piece, shown publicly in the gallery as its title. A piece name is required when you submit.
  • Optional artist name. If you provide it, it is shown publicly in the gallery as the credit for your piece. It is optional; leave it blank and no artist is attributed.
  • Optional email address. Only if you opt in by entering it when you submit art. It is used solely to email you once when your piece has been physically printed. See "How we protect your email" below.
  • Technical and abuse-prevention data. Your IP address and basic request metadata are processed transiently to enforce rate limits and prevent abuse of the service. We do not build advertising profiles and we do not use tracking cookies.
  • Camera recording of the display. The camera records the physical flip-disc display drawing your artwork — it films the device, not you. This recording is uploaded to YouTube as a public video and embedded on this site.

How we use your information

  • To display your artwork on the physical device and in the public gallery.
  • To record and publish the print to YouTube and embed it on this site.
  • To send you a one-time completion email, only if you opted in.
  • To moderate submissions so the public device and gallery stay appropriate.
  • To protect the service from abuse (rate limiting).

How we protect your email

Your email address is never published, never shown in the gallery, and never returned by any public part of this website. It is encrypted at rest and stored outside the web-accessible area of our server, keyed by an anonymous identifier. It is used only to send you the single "your piece is done" notification, and then it is deleted. We never sell it, rent it, or use it for marketing.

What is public

Because P.A.R. is a shared public art device, the following are visible to anyone: your pixel artwork, the piece name you provide (and an artist name, if you choose to add one), and the YouTube recording of your print. Do not include information in those fields that you would not want public. Your email address is never part of this.

YouTube API Services

P.A.R. uses YouTube API Services to upload the recording of each print to YouTube and to display upload status on this site. By using P.A.R. you are also agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. Information handled through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy. P.A.R. uploads videos to its own YouTube channels; it does not access, read, or store data from your Google account, and it does not ask you to sign in with Google.

Data retention

Artwork, piece names, artist names, and the associated YouTube recordings are retained as part of the public gallery. An opted-in email address is deleted after the completion notification is sent. Abuse-prevention data is short-lived and is not retained beyond what is needed to enforce rate limits.

Your choices

  • Email: providing an email is entirely optional. To not receive a completion notification, simply leave the email field blank when you submit.
  • Public fields: only include information you are comfortable making public in your artwork, piece name, and artist name. Because P.A.R. is a shared public art device, submissions and their recordings become part of the permanent public gallery and are not removable on request.

Children

P.A.R. is a general-audience art project and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
